PROFILE PHOTO
=============

Drop your professional portrait here and name it exactly:

    profile.jpg

(square image works best — e.g. 600 x 600 px or larger)

Until that file exists, the hero card automatically shows a
stylish "RYD" initials placeholder, so the site never looks broken.

PNG instead of JPG? Either rename it to profile.jpg, or open
index.html and change  src="assets/profile.jpg"  to your filename
(look in the Hero section, around the .profile-img tag).
